Our Story

Flavours That Cross Borders

Located on the main tourist strip near the famous Batu Ferringhi Night Market, D'Feringghi Tandoori House brings you an exquisite fusion of Pakistani, Indian, Arabian, and Western cuisines — all prepared fresh in our traditional clay tandoor oven and charcoal grill.

Whether you're visiting after a day at ESCAPE theme park, Entopia, or the Tropical Spice Garden, our restaurant is the perfect dining oasis. Over 10 years serving tourists and locals alike with Halal, MSG-free dishes and plenty of vegan, gluten-free, and dairy-free options.
